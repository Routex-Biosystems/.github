# Routex Biosys

Quantifying biological pathway activity with targeted RNA panels and AI/ML.

## About us

Routex Biosys is a globally focused deep-tech biotechnology startup. We analyze biological pathway activity with targeted RNA panels and AI/ML models, making complex biological processes quantitative and understandable.

## What we do

Biological processes are complex and hard to read from a single marker. We measure the activity of biological pathways with targeted RNA panels, then use AI and machine learning models to turn these measurements into clear, quantitative readouts that researchers can interpret and compare.

## Our focus

- Pathway activity analysis with targeted RNA panels
- AI and machine learning models for quantitative interpretation
- Oncology research as our starting point

## Our first product

We are developing our first product for the Research Use Only (RUO) market.

## Research Use Only

Our products are intended for research use only and are not intended for use in diagnostic procedures.
